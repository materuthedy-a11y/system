# Mizania brand package

**Product name:** Mizania Business Suite  
**Tagline:** Rekodi · Fuatilia · Endelea  
**Meaning:** Keep business records, follow activity, and keep the business moving.

## Brand mark

The Mizania symbol keeps the open ledger book, redrawn with a stronger center spine, balanced pages, crisp record lines, and a small rising page-corner detail. It represents keeping and following business records while suggesting steady progress. The mark is designed to stay recognizable at favicon and mobile-app sizes.

## Logo files

- `logos/svg/wordmark-light.svg` and `wordmark-dark.svg` — horizontal logo for light and dark surfaces.
- `logos/svg/wordmark.svg` — primary horizontal wordmark.
- `logos/svg/stacked-light.svg` and `stacked-dark.svg` — centered/stacked logo.
- `logos/svg/symbol.svg` — gold ledger mark on a black tile.
- `logos/svg/symbol-monochrome-black.svg` and `symbol-monochrome-white.svg` — single-color mark variants.
- `logos/png/mizania-wordmark-light.png`, `mizania-wordmark-dark.png`, `mizania-stacked-light.png`, `mizania-stacked-dark.png` — raster lockups.
- `logos/png/mizania-app-icon-*.png` — application icons at common device sizes.
- `pwa/mizania-maskable-192x192.png`, `mizania-maskable-512x512.png`, `apple-touch-icon-180x180.png` — install icons.
- `generate_assets.py` — source generator for the SVG and PNG logo assets.

## Colors

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mizania gold | `#D7AE45` | Primary logo mark and brand highlights |
| Light gold | `#F0D98F` | Fine details on dark surfaces |
| Deep gold | `#A77A1D` | Tagline and small text on light surfaces |
| Ink black | `#111214` | Logo tile and primary dark ink |
| Charcoal | `#1B1C1F` | Supporting dark surfaces |
| Warm white | `#F7F4EC` | Wordmark text on dark surfaces |

Gold and black define the Mizania product identity. Existing application page colors and semantic status colors remain separate from this brand package.

The product brand is separate from each business using the system. Business names, customer records, tenant IDs, authentication keys, and API routes remain business/application data and are not renamed by this brand update.
